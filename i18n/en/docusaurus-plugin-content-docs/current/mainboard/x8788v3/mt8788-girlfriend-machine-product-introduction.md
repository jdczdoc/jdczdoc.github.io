# Product Introduction

The MT8788 girlfriend-machine mainboard is an Android mainboard for audio and video products based on the MediaTek MT8788 platform. It is independently developed, manufactured, and sold by Shenzhen 9Tripod Technology Co., Ltd. MediaTek MT8788, also known as i500, is a high-performance and low-power processor built on TSMC 12nm process technology, with an octa-core architecture of quad A73 and quad A53.

![MT8788 girlfriend-machine mainboard appearance](./mt8788-board-overview.png)

## Feature Highlights

- Processor platform: MT8788 / MT8385V / MT8183, Android 13.
- CPU: octa-core architecture with quad A73 + quad A53, up to 2GHz.
- Memory: 2GB / 4GB / 8GB LPDDR4.
- Storage: 4GB / 8GB / 16GB / 32GB / 64GB / 128GB eMMC options.
- USB: two external Type-A USB 2.0 ports, one Type-C USB 2.0 port, and four internal USB 2.0 wafer connectors.
- Display: one HDMI input and one LVDS output, supporting 21.5~86-inch 1920 x 1080 FHD LVDS panels.
- Camera: one MIPI 4-lane CSI camera connector; USB camera solutions are also supported.
- Audio: 3.5mm headphone output, external 2 x 3~15W speaker connector, and dual MIC input.
- Expansion: debug UART, Light/NFC, RTC, IR, backlight, touch, key, and battery-level reading interfaces.

## Application Scope

This product is an Android mainboard designed for girlfriend-machine / smart display products. It is suitable for 15.6-inch to 32-inch LVDS screens and can also be adapted for larger display terminals. Typical scenarios include home entertainment, restaurant ordering, education, split-screen office use, product display, advertising playback, fitness, digital photo frames, games, video conferencing, electronic whiteboards, and digital signage.
