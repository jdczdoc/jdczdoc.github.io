# I527BV3 Development Board

I527BV3 is a T527/A527-series development board using the X527CV2 core module. It targets AIoT, industrial control, digital signage, video input/output and edge-computing applications.

## Quick facts

- Source board name: `i527bv3`
- SoC: Allwinner T527/T527N/A527 series, octa-core Arm Cortex-A55
- Core module: X527CV2, 200 castellated pins, 55 mm × 55 mm
- Board size: 150 mm × 102 mm × 1.6 mm
- Input: 12 V DC, 3 A recommended
- Memory: 2 GB / 4 GB LPDDR4X
- Display: HDMI OUT, HDMI IN, eDP, LVDS and MIPI DSI
- OS: Android 13/14 and Linux, depending on the released SDK
