## Chapter 1: I527 Development Board Introduction

### 1.1 Product Introduction
The I527BV3 is a development board based on the Allwinner T527 CPU, independently developed, manufactured, and sold by Shenzhen JiuDing ChuangZhan Technology Co., Ltd.  
The I527 development board is a high-performance embedded development platform based on the ARM Cortex-A55 processor, widely used in IoT and industrial control applications. It features rich interfaces such as GPIO, UART, I2C, SPI, and USB, supports LCD and touch screens, and provides stable power management and various development tools. Its efficient processing power and flexible scalability make it an ideal choice for developing real-time systems and smart applications.

### 1.3 Board Dimensions
- **Dimensions**: 150mm × 102mm × 1.6mm  
- **Operating Temperature**: -40~85°C or 0~70°C  
- **Storage Temperature**: -10~50°C

### 1.3.1 Specifications
- **System Configuration**  
  - CPU: T527/A527 (ARM Cortex-A55)  
  - Clock Speed: 1.8GHz  
  - RAM: 2GB or 4GB  
  - ROM: 4GB/8GB/16GB/32GB/64GB  
  - Power IC: AXP717B, supports dynamic frequency scaling  

- **Interface Parameters**  
  - Power: DC input, 12V/3A  
  - LVDS Interface: 2 LVDS interfaces, 1 multiplexed with MIPI interface  
  - Touch Interface: Capacitive touch, I2C interface  
  - Audio Interface: IIS/PCM/PDM/SPDIF  
  - SD Card Interface: 1 SDIO output channel  
  - eMMC Interface: Onboard eMMC interface, pins not separately routed out  
  - Ethernet Interface: Supports 1 Gigabit Ethernet interface  
  - USB HOST 2.0 Interface: 2  
  - USB HOST 3.0 Interface: 1  
  - UART Interface: 2  
  - CAN Interface: 2  
  - RS485 Interface: 1  
  - RS232 Interface: 1  
  - Camera Interface: 1 MIPI-CSI  
  - HDMI OUT Interface: 1  
  - HDMI IN Interface: 1, via LT6911C  
  - PCIe Interface: 1
